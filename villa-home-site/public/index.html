The Villa

Where teakwood and
jungle breathe together

Set behind quiet garden walls in Batu Belig, Villa Home is a tribute to traditional Balinese craftsmanship — a hand-built joglo of aged teak, soaring alang-alang roofs and open pavilions that dissolve the line between inside and out.

Days drift between the long emerald pool, shaded daybeds and a vast open-air living space carved with antique detail. Spread across 1,200 m² of tropical garden, the villa offers complete privacy for a family or group of up to nine, yet sits a short stroll from Batu Belig beach and minutes from Seminyak's dining and boutiques.

This is Bali the way it was meant to be felt — barefoot, unhurried, and quietly luxurious.

The Spaces

Room to breathe, indoors and out

The Heart

The Joglo Living Pavilion

The soul of the villa — a vast open-sided joglo with hand-carved ceilings, deep daybeds and lounge seating that opens straight onto the pool and garden. The perfect place to gather for sundowners or stretch out with a book in the breeze.

  • Open-air lounge
  • Carved teak ceiling
  • Garden & pool views

Outdoors

Pool & Tropical Garden

A long, sun-drenched pool sits at the centre of 1,200 m² of lush garden, edged with loungers, a shaded bale and a relaxed outdoor lounge beneath a Balinese parasol. Space to swim, sunbathe and disappear into green.

  • Large private pool
  • Sun loungers
  • Garden lounge & bale

Rest

Three Bedroom Suites

Three air-conditioned bedrooms blend antique Balinese doors and artwork with crisp linens and ensuite bathrooms. One opens directly to the pool, another frames the garden through full-height glass — each its own private retreat. Extra beds are available to sleep up to nine.

  • En-suite bathrooms
  • Air-conditioned
  • Sleeps up to 9

Gather

Open-Air Kitchen & Dining

A fully equipped open kitchen with a generous timber island flows into the living pavilion — made for long, leisurely meals, morning coffee in the garden air and evenings spent together under the joglo roof.

  • Full kitchen
  • Timber island
  • Indoor-outdoor dining
