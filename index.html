The Villa

A modern Bali sanctuary, traditionally crafted

Set behind quiet garden walls in leafy Batu Belig, Villa Home is a tribute to traditional Balinese craftsmanship — a hand-built joglo of aged teak and soaring alang-alang roofs, where open pavilions dissolve the line between inside and out.

Days drift between the long emerald pool, shaded daybeds and a vast open-air living space carved with antique detail. The villa offers complete privacy for a family or group of up to nine, yet sits a short stroll from Batu Belig beach and minutes from Seminyak's dining and boutiques.

This is Bali the way it was meant to be felt — barefoot, unhurried, quietly luxurious.

The Heart

The open-air pavilion

The soul of the villa is a vast open-sided joglo — hand-carved teak ceilings, deep daybeds and lounge seating that opens straight onto the pool and garden. The perfect place to gather for sundowners or stretch out with a book in the breeze.

  • Open-air lounge & dining
  • Hand-carved teak ceiling
  • Pool & garden views
Outdoors

Pool & tropical garden

A long, sun-drenched emerald pool sits at the centre of 1,200 m² of lush garden, edged with loungers, fringed parasols and a shaded bale. Space to swim, sunbathe and disappear into green.

  • Long private pool
  • Loungers & daybeds
  • 1,200 m² tropical garden
Rest

Three vaulted bedrooms

Three air-conditioned bedroom suites blend antique Balinese doors and artwork with crisp linens and ensuite bathrooms — each crowned with a soaring teak rosette ceiling. One opens directly to the pool, another frames the garden through full-height glass.

  • 3 en-suite bedrooms
  • Sleeps up to 9 with extra beds
  • Soaring vaulted teak ceilings
Bathe

Terracotta sanctuaries

Hand-finished tadelakt plaster in deep terracotta, antique mother-of-pearl inlaid mirrors, stone vessel sinks and brass fixtures — each bathroom is its own small theatre. Some open to a private outdoor rain shower beneath a bamboo roof.

  • Hand-finished plaster walls
  • Stone vessel sinks
  • Outdoor rain showers
